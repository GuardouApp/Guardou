# Support the Project

The simplest way to contribute is by using Guardou and reporting any [issues](https://github.com/GuardouApp/Guardou/issues/new/choose) you may find.

## Development

Programmers familiar with C# or Flutter can contribute by fixing open issues, and by discussing and implementing features.

## Donate

You can also [donate a small amount](https://ko-fi.com/grochoska) to help with the costs of developing Guardou.
Donations will fund costs such as the developer accounts required by Apple ($99/year) and Google ($25 one-time).
